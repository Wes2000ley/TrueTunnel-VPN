# Code of Conduct

TrueTunnel aims to be a welcoming, technically rigorous community. This code
is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful, patient, and constructive;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- acknowledging uncertainty and different experiences; and
- accepting feedback and correcting mistakes in good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or attention, doxxing, threats,
publishing private information without permission, and disruptive conduct in
project spaces. Do not post credentials, tunnel secrets, private keys, or
other sensitive material while reporting a problem.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify, edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
limit participation when conduct violates this code. They will use their best
judgment, respect privacy where possible, and consider the context and
severity of an incident. There is no guaranteed response time.

To report a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer through a private
channel already provided by the repository or project profile. If no private
channel is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route
without naming or describing sensitive details. Do not use public issues to
publish personal information or security reports; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for vulnerabilities.
